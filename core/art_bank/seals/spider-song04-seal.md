# P1 — SONG 4 SEAL (Paramarthasara vv29–37)

*The seal brief: complete justification of the image — symbolism, colour,
meaning — built so no element can drift and no mistake can be made.

## 0. What the seal is

One vertical frame, read bottom→top, that carries the song's argument
and the parable's five beats and the rasa's movement in a single
image: **the knot that contains its own light, unwrapping into the sun.**

## 1. Why a picture can seal the text (hermetic logic)

Hermetic/alchemical art never decorates — it *compresses argument into
symbol*, then reads it by rule:

- **Correspondence (as above / so below).** The Hermetic core: what
  is small mirrors what is great. The seal uses this as its ENGINE —
  the cold blue star inside the knot IS the same light as the great
  gold sun above, differing only in concealment. Which is the song's
  exact doctrine: recognition, not acquisition — *nothing is added*
  (the universal thesis the whole work defends).
- **Sequence-as-axis.** Solis plates stage alchemical stages as
  separable scenes; our seal compresses the sequence into ONE rising
  axis — the reading direction itself is the argument (delusion →
  turning → unveiling). Time becomes space.
- **The border/commentary rule.** In Solis pages the border comments
  on the plate; here the *frame architecture* (arch-topped gold) does
  the commenting — the arch is the parable's "fourth tent, the one
  with no canvas": structure itself becomes doctrine.
- **No naked symbol (Tattva-shaiva and hermetic alike).** Every
  symbol is double-anchored: once to the Sanskrit verse, once to the
  C1 essence. If an element cannot cite both, it is removed — this is
  the anti-mistake machine.

## 4. Composition laws (from the 3 failed drafts)

1. **All four zones in one frame, physically connected.** (v2 lost the
   web when I let a thread replace it; v3 fussed; v4 obeyed.)
2. **Exactly one spider.** Duplicates echo meaning (v2's roundel bug).
3. **The web is a KNOT, not a doily.** Pretty radial webs read as
   decoration; the constricted choked ball reads as bondage.
4. **No roundels, no medallion template, no named template parts.**
   Reference carries structure; the prompt carries only the seal.
5. **Reference = a Solis page not yet used.** Selection rule: bordered
   page > cropped plate; scene-affinity to the parable; clean/hi-res;
   seed logged. user1 is now spent for this parable (v4's page).
6. **The seal text must itself be a mandala** — vivid, complete,
   self-contained: reference carries *style*, prompt carries *meaning*.

## 6. The P1 prompt as run (v4, seed 123, guidance 3.5)

> In the manner of this manuscript page, one vertical seal: at the
> bottom, black gnarled roots knotted into a choking tangle; among
> those roots a spider web, strung tight and constricted, choked with
> tangled threads and old debris, glistening with caught dewdrops like
> imprisoned stars; from the web's dark heart one gossamer thread
> climbs, and the spider — one only — weaves herself up along it, legs
> strung to the line, leaving the knot below; above her the sky opens
> into pure gold-white light, and she ascends into it, small and
> blazing, free. Roots hold the web. Web holds the thread. Thread holds
> the light. Colors: umber and black at the base, lapis and ivory
> mid-air, burning gold at the crown. Pity below, peace above. No text,
> no letters, one spider total.

Why it worked: structure outsourced to the reference; the prompt
contained ONLY meaning; the triad (roots→web→light) stated as rhythm;
rasa summed in six words. Everything else above exists to REGENERATE
this outcome deliberately for the next parable — different reference,
different world, same incorruptible logic.
